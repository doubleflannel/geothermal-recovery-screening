# Public-dataset method analogs

This repository is framed as a small public-data method demonstration, not as a
large geothermal database paper. The relevant comparison is therefore other
papers that demonstrate a method, benchmark, standardization layer, or screening
framework across a handful of public datasets.

## Why the count is reasonable

The active geothermal screening set uses six threshold-compatible
thermal-recovery routes plus one below-threshold control. That is the same
order of public-dataset scale as adjacent method papers that establish
credibility across 3, 4, 5, 7, or 22 public datasets or record groups.

| Analog | Public dataset count | Why it is comparable |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Miranda et al. 2026 geothermal TRT/deep-screening | 22 TRT datasets plus supporting records | Public or industry thermal records are screened, quality-assessed, converted to a common proxy, and mapped. |
| Kratzert et al. 2023 Caravan hydrology | 7 open hydrology source datasets | Multiple public datasets are standardized into one comparable schema. |
| VMMCD 2025 remote-sensing change detection | 3 public datasets | A method is tested across a small curated public benchmark set. |
| AFL-Net 2023 remote-sensing building extraction | 4 public datasets | A method is tested across several public regions and benchmark settings. |
| Remote-sensing image captioning with DINOv3/Transformer fusion | 5 public datasets | A small public-dataset panel is used to test generality across geospatial datasets. |

## Claim boundary

This comparison supports the scale of the demonstration, not the strength of
any individual permeability estimate. The repository still has to label each
geothermal route by its own evidence: validated / qualified, discovery-only,
source-request-needed, metadata-limited, or support-only.

Do not describe the current geothermal set as a large-scale database, an
exhaustive public-data survey, or six validated datasets. The safer statement
is:

> The first demonstration applies the evidence ladder to six
> threshold-compatible public thermal-recovery routes and one below-threshold
> control, a scale comparable to adjacent method papers that establish
> credibility across a handful of public benchmark datasets rather than through
> exhaustive surveying.
